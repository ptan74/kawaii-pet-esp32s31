# app_pet_market

Markets and business headlines for the Info screen.

- **Quotes:** Yahoo Finance chart data (no key) for S&P 500, gold, USD/SGD and the large firms Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, Amazon and JPMorgan. The change is versus the previous close.
- **Headlines:** BBC business RSS feed (no key), first four items.

Refresh every 5 minutes; retry after 60 seconds when the news fetch fails. The callback runs on the task.
Data is for information only, not financial advice.
